# ig-grow references

What Instagram itself has said about reach to non-followers. Checked
2026-10-15. Primary sources first. Where only secondary coverage exists, it
says so. Anything not listed here is not claimed in SKILL.md.

## Ranking signals: watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach

- Adam Mosseri, Instagram post, January 2025:
  https://www.instagram.com/p/DFFyRp-pINJ/
  As reported by Social Media Today, 22 Jan 2025:
  https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-2025/738034/
  "The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes
  and sends." "Pay close attention to average watch time, likes per reach,
  and sends per reach." "Likes are slightly more important for connected
  content, and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content."
  (Connected = followers. Unconnected = recommendations to non-followers.)
  Secondary report of a primary video. The figure "sends are 3 to 5x likes"
  seen on blogs has no primary source and is not used.

- Instagram, "Instagram Ranking Explained" (31 May 2023):
  https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained
  For Reels, the key predictions include how likely you are to "reshare a
  reel, watch a reel all the way through, like it, and go to the audio
  page." Instagram makes "low-resolution or watermarked reels" and "reels
  that have already been posted on Instagram" less visible.

## How a reel reaches non-followers

- Instagram Creators, "Helping creators of all sizes break through"
  (26 Feb 2025):
  https://creators.instagram.com/blog/helping-creators-of-all-sizes-break-through
  Among non-followers, a reel is first shown to a small audience likely to
  enjoy it. The best performers are then shown to progressively wider
  audiences. Distribution follows engagement, not follower count.

- Instagram Creators, "Finding success on reels in 2025" (21 Jan 2025):
  https://creators.instagram.com/blog/the-latest-with-instagram
  The most effective way to grow is to post original content consistently.
  Reels up to 3 minutes can be recommended to non-followers (US first).
  Adding audio to photos and carousels can get them into the Reels tab.
  Reels get a reply bar so viewers can start conversations. Announces Edits,
  Instagram's video app, with watermark-free export and real-time insights.

## Originality

- Instagram Creators, "Rewarding original creators on Instagram"
  (30 Apr 2026):
  https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram
  Extends originality rules from Reels to photos and carousels. Aggregators
  are no longer eligible for recommendations. Re-uploading someone else's
  content, or only adding a border, watermark, subtitles or credit, does not
  count as original. Eligibility returns when most recent posts over a
  30-day period are original. Check standing in Account Status.

- Meta, "2026: AI Drives Performance" (28 Jan 2026):
  https://about.fb.com/news/2026/01/2026-ai-drives-performance/
  Instagram "increased the prevalence of original content in the US by 10
  percentage points in Q4", "with 75% of recommendations now coming from
  original posts."

## Trial Reels

- Meta, "Test Content With Non-Followers Using Trial Reels" (10 Dec 2024,
  updated 26 Jun 2025):
  https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first-see-what-perfoms-best/
  Trial reels are shown to people who do not follow you. About 24 hours
  later you see views, likes, comments and shares. You can auto-share to
  followers if it performs well on views in the first 72 hours.
- Instagram Creators blog on Trial Reels:
  https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels
- Meta's own figure (June 2025, via secondary coverage, not re-verified on
  a primary page): of 400,000+ creators who tried Trial Reels, 80% saw more
  non-follower reach. Treat as Meta's data, not a guarantee.

## Collabs

- Meta, "New Ways to Create With Music and Collaborate With Friends on
  Instagram" (11 Aug 2023):
  https://about.fb.com/news/2023/08/music-and-collabs-on-instagram/
  "Once they accept, the content will reach each collaborator's audience and
  appear on each account's profile grid." Collaborator limits have changed
  since (3 in 2023, later Instagram pages say 5). Check the app.

## Reply to a comment with a reel

- Instagram Help, "Reply to a comment on your reel with a new reel":
  https://help.instagram.com/249714943742850/
- Instagram for Creators FAQ: https://creators.instagram.com/faq?locale=en_US
  Tap reply on a comment, then the camera icon. The comment becomes a
  sticker in the new reel.

## Search

- Instagram, "Breaking Down How Instagram Search Works" (25 Aug 2021):
  https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/break-down-how-instagram-search-works
  "For a post to be found in Search, put keywords and hashtags in the
  caption, not the comments." Use a handle or profile name related to your
  content. "Make sure your bio includes keywords about who you are and what
  your profile is about." Old but not superseded by anything newer found.

## Posting frequency

- Instagram Creators (21 Jan 2025, above): post original content
  consistently. No official weekly number was found on an Instagram page.
  Popular figures ("3 to 5 feed posts a week", "2 stories a day") trace to
  secondary reports of a 2021 Mosseri talk. The 4-a-week target in grow.py
  is this pack's working default (from /ig-plan), not an Instagram rule.

## Fake growth

- Instagram, "Reducing Inauthentic Activity on Instagram" (19 Nov 2018):
  https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/reducing-inauthentic-activity-on-instagram
  "We will begin removing inauthentic likes, follows and comments from
  accounts that use third-party apps" to "artificially grow their audience."
  Bought followers, follow/unfollow bots, pods and automated likes, follows,
  comments or DMs break Instagram's Terms and Community Guidelines and get
  accounts restricted.
